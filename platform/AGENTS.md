## No dashboard — deliberate

The dashboard app (`app/(app)/`, components/, lesson reader, community panel)
was deliberately deleted in July 2026. Do not rebuild it. The platform is only:
auth (`(auth)/`, `auth/callback`), `/api/step-feedback`, static step pages in
`public/steps/` (generated by `_dev/step-generator/` at the repo root), and the
`/steps` index. Students arrive via login-gated step links posted in Slack.
