## Findings worth keeping

- **A 20px SVG mark shot at dsf 8 is a 160px raster, and a slide that fills
  the safe box with it is a 5x enlargement** — the same softness the 8-07
  single faces were rejected for, arriving from the shot rather than from the
  artwork. The `flagx` block re-shoots single glyphs at dsf 16 in a short
  viewport (width stays 402 — width is what decides the mobile layout).
- **The callout bodies are relabelled per TEXT NODE, not per authored
  string.** A body with a `**bold**` span is two or three text nodes, so each
  node is its own MAP row — mapping the whole authored sentence matches
  nothing and ships the original silently. Three callouts' nodes are in
  `neutralize.mjs` under a dated comment.
- **Shoot the tree's folder from a step INSIDE it.** The drawer opens the
  current step's own ancestors, so standing in a unit puts that unit's whole
  subtree in the crop; standing on the orientation step leaves every other
  unit as one quiet collapsed row.
- **The anatomy plate's rules were measured, not eyeballed**: canvas
  `actualBoundingBoxAscent` for "B" and "s" against an inline baseline probe,
  at the exact size and line-height the plate uses. The eyeballed first cut
  was 8px out on every rule.
