How money in gets split

Every time money comes in, it is divided across the buckets by these percentages. Locked buckets are left out of "safe to spend". Changing the split only affects money that arrives after the change.

The daily routine

  1. At the end of the day, open your MoMo app and take the transaction history or download the statement (CSV, PDF or screenshots all work).
  2. Send it to Claude in chat and say "update my buckets".
  3. Claude adds each transaction here, splits money in across the buckets and puts each payment in the bucket it came out of. Anything it is unsure about lands in Unsorted for you to pick.
  4. Check "safe to spend today" before you spend tomorrow.
